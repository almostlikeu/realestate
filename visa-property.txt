            <h2 class="serif text-3xl text-gray-900 mb-6 border-b pb-4">1. The 10-Year UAE Golden Visa</h2>
            <p class="mb-6">The UAE Golden Visa is a long-term residence visa which enables foreign talents to live, work, or study in the UAE while enjoying exclusive benefits. For real estate investors, it represents the most highly sought-after residency option.</p>
            
            <div class="bg-gray-50 p-6 rounded-sm border border-gray-200 mb-8">
                <h3 class="font-semibold text-gray-900 mb-4 uppercase tracking-wider text-sm">Key Requirements:</h3>
                <ul class="space-y-3">
                    <li><i class="fas fa-check text-[--brand-teal] mr-2"></i><strong>Minimum Investment:</strong> AED 2,000,000 (approx. $545,000 USD).</li>
                    <li><i class="fas fa-check text-[--brand-teal] mr-2"></i><strong>Property Type:</strong> Can be ready/secondary market or off-plan properties.</li>
                    <li><i class="fas fa-check text-[--brand-teal] mr-2"></i><strong>Mortgage Rule:</strong> The property can be mortgaged, provided the bank provides a NOC or if the investor's equity meets the 2M threshold.</li>
                </ul>
            </div>

            <h2 class="serif text-3xl text-gray-900 mb-6 border-b pb-4 mt-12">2. The 2-Year Property Investor Visa</h2>
            <p class="mb-6">For investors looking for a more accessible entry point into the Dubai market, the 2-Year Investor Visa provides renewable residency rights linked to property ownership.</p>
            
            <div class="bg-gray-50 p-6 rounded-sm border border-gray-200 mb-8">
                <h3 class="font-semibold text-gray-900 mb-4 uppercase tracking-wider text-sm">Key Requirements:</h3>
                <ul class="space-y-3">
                    <li><i class="fas fa-check text-[--brand-teal] mr-2"></i><strong>Minimum Investment:</strong> AED 750,000 (approx. $205,000 USD).</li>
                    <li><i class="fas fa-check text-[--brand-teal] mr-2"></i><strong>Property Type:</strong> Must be a ready, residential property (off-plan is not eligible for this specific tier).</li>
                    <li><i class="fas fa-check text-[--brand-teal] mr-2"></i><strong>Spouse & Dependents:</strong> Allows the investor to sponsor their family members.</li>
                </ul>
